## Scope feasibility
A balanced, positive-sequence, series-line AC power-flow solver is a feasible educational scope for seven days if the student can work daily and obtain topic approval. Dense Jacobians favor clarity over large-network speed. Synthetic five-bus data avoid unsupported benchmark claims. Reactive limits, taps, line charging, unbalanced systems, dynamics and OPF are excluded. Seven days is a study plan, not a guarantee of mastery or supervisor approval.
